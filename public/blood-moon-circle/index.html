Frequently Asked Questions

How do I wash the Aethel-Silk reusable pads? +
Rinse used pads under cool tap water until runoff is clear, toss them into your included Sanctuary Wash Net, and wash on a standard gentle cycle with mild detergent. Hang to dry or tumble dry low. Never use fabric softeners or bleach.
How do the Vali (Heat) and Kuli (Cold) thermal packs work? +
Take the cotton muslin pack out of the Geckoorul's belly. Microwave Vali with 1/2 cup of water for 45–60 seconds, or store Kuli in your freezer inside its dedicated Frost Sleeve. Slip the pack back into the kangaroo pouch and button closed.
Is the Geckoorul plushie microwave-safe? +
Only the 100% natural cotton muslin inner pack (Vali) goes into the microwave. The outer plushie stays safely on your bed, ready to receive the warm pack.
Will the pads fit a young teen having her first period? +
Yes! Each pad features dual KAM plastic snaps on the wings, allowing the snapped width to adjust down to 2.5 inches to fit narrow youth underwear snugly without twisting.
Can I pause, change tiers, or cancel anytime? +
Yes. You can swap between the Raven and Siren tiers, update your address, skip a month, or cancel anytime with 1 click in your account portal.
